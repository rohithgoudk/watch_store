Craftsmanship

Every movement, opened by hand

Each Meridian caliber passes through eleven pairs of hands before it reaches a wrist — regulated, oiled, and cased in our Columbus workshop, the same way it was done in 1998.

We still use loupe and tweezer where machines would be faster, because faster isn't the point. Accuracy, and the decades a well-built watch should outlast its owner, is.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before ordering.

Every Meridian watch ships with a 5-year international warranty covering the movement, case, and crystal against manufacturing defects.

Unworn watches can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Servicing and engraved pieces are covered under separate terms.
